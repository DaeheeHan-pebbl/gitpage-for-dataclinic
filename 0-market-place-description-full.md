**Pebblous Data Clinic | AI Training Data Quality**

**Spot redundant samples, outliers, and potential coverage gaps in your AI training data.** Get visual diagnosis reports with recommendations on what to review, remove, or collect next. A data quality diagnosis service by Pebblous.

**Overview**

Pebblous Data Clinic helps teams understand the quality of their AI training datasets and prioritize improvements. Baseline checks examine dataset consistency, missing values, class balance, and other statistical characteristics. DataLens goes further by mapping sample similarity and density, making it easier to review repetitive clusters, outliers, and potential coverage gaps that class counts alone can miss.

Data Clinic applies selected data quality measures from ISO/IEC 5259-2. The diagnosis report combines documented findings with visual evidence and recommendations so teams can decide what to review, remove, or collect next.

Pebblous has applied its data quality technology in projects with Hyundai Motor and the Republic of Korea Army. Data Clinic has also been supplied to public institutions through Korea's Public Procurement Service (PPS).

**Highlights**

- **See beyond class counts.** Review potential coverage gaps, repetitive clusters, and outliers through visual diagnostics powered by DataLens.
- **Measure data quality consistently.** Assess datasets using selected ISO/IEC 5259-2 data quality measures, with documented results to support review and comparison.
- **Plan your next improvement.** Use diagnostic reports to prioritize data review, removal, and collection. Custom data improvement projects are available through Enterprise engagements.
